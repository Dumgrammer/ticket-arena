Danger zone Stuck tickets

A ticket is stuck when its PR has sat open in review, or open for promotion to staging, past its limit (2 days in review, 2 in promotion). Merged and staged tickets never count. A refresh that finds a newly stuck ticket also sends you a notification.

        Where these numbers come from
        • Tickets and stages come from your 50 most recent PRs in your repo, read with the GitHub CLI by a refresh that runs every 15 minutes inside the Ticket Arena container. Stage is worked out from each PR's target branch and state, and from teammates' "Promote #PR" PRs, not from Jira.
        • Days in stage count from when the PR was opened (review, promoting) or merged (main, staging). The stuck limit is 2 days for review and 2 for promotion (config.json).
        • Jira status, sprint and story points are not shown: everything comes from GitHub.
        • Comments come from your open PRs only. Copilot quota notices are left out.
